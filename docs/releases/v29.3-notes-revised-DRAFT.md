# V29.3 release notes (revised wording, draft)

Status: draft, proposed replacement wording for the original V29.3 release notes
(`docs/V29.3-RELEASE-NOTES.md`). It describes the same source-only release,
tag `v29.3`. No new tag, no APK. This repository distributes no APKs.

Rambler Bangla is in development. V29.3 is a maintainer test build. The
points below describe intended behavior and what was checked, not a stable
guarantee.

## What V29.3 aims to do

- **One language per sentence in multilingual voice typing.** On the
  multilingual layout (abc → বাংলা), each dictated sentence is meant to come
  out in Bangla script or in English, not mixed.
- **Table-based Bangla spellings for English words.** English words spoken
  inside Bangla are meant to come out as transliterations (কোম্পানি,
  টেলিভিশন, বার্লিন) instead of letter-by-letter output. The original notes
  report a blind check of 85 of 200 exact (17 of 200 before). Expect misses.
- **Protected text.** #hashtags, @handles, links, emails and times are meant
  to stay in Latin letters on the voice paths covered by the test corpus.
- **Clipboard drag-to-reorder.** In development; the live reorder can swing
  too far sideways while an item is held.

## What was checked

- Host test suite: 292-case corpus across the three layouts, passed at
  release time (`tests/v29.3/`). This is a historical result, not proof for a
  current signed build.
- Static suite on a synthetic fixture (`tests/run_tests.sh`): policy
  assertions and end-to-end patch checks, including fail-closed negatives.
- Real device: not established in this repository for the current build.
  The original notes mention device confirmation of the Bangla-only,
  English-only and multilingual layouts, with misses in quick mid-sentence
  switches. Treat device behavior as unverified until a new on-phone run is
  recorded.

## Known limits

- Very short Romanized Bangla sentences (three words or fewer, all
  colloquial) can stay in Latin letters.
- A Bangla clause and an English clause spoken as one sentence with no pause
  follow the majority language.
- Gboard's combined "EN · BN" keyboard reports itself as English (US), so
  Bangla spoken there stays in Latin letters. Use abc → বাংলা for mixed
  dictation.

## Settings

- **Bangla script correction** (on by default): when off, Rambler converts
  nothing and you get Gboard's own output.
- **Rambler diagnostics** (off by default): for bug reports only. The saved
  log includes dictated or typed text before and after conversion and stays on
  the phone unless shared. Leave it off in normal use.
